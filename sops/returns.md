# Standard Operating Procedure: Returns & Exchanges

## 1. Eligibility & Conditions
- Customers may return or exchange items within 15 days of the in-store purchase date or online delivery date[cite: 3].
- Returned items must be unused, unwashed, and undamaged, with all original tags, labels, and packaging intact[cite: 3].
- A valid proof of purchase (original invoice/bill or order confirmation) is required for standard returns[cite: 3].
- **Missing Receipt Escalation:** Returns requested without a valid receipt cannot be processed by cashiers and must be escalated to the Store Manager.

## 2. Non-Returnable Items
- For hygiene, safety, or pricing reasons, the following categories cannot be returned or exchanged unless found defective at delivery: innerwear, lingerie, socks, cosmetics, personal-care products, FMCG products, gift cards/vouchers, altered/customized items, and EOSS/clearance merchandise[cite: 3, 4].

## 3. Damaged or Defective Products
- Customers reporting defective, damaged, or incorrect online deliveries must contact the store/support within 48 hours of delivery for a full refund or replacement upon verification[cite: 4].
- **In-Store Damaged Returns:** Any damaged item brought to the counter requires physical inspection and explicit approval from the Store Manager before a refund or replacement is issued.

## 4. Refund Methods & Bundled Offers
- In-store purchases are eligible for exchanges or store credit; cash refunds are only issued where required by law or at management's discretion[cite: 4].
- Online purchase refunds are credited to the original payment method within 7–10 business days after inspection[cite: 4].
- **Promotional & B2G1 Returns:** If an item bought under a discount or "Buy 2 Get 1" (B2G1) offer is returned, the discount is prorated and the benefit recalculated[cite: 4, 6]. If returning an item breaks the B2G1 qualifying set, the value of the free item (always the lowest-priced item) is deducted from the refund, or the free item must be returned[cite: 6]. Used promotional coupons are voided and not re-issued upon return[cite: 5, 7].
