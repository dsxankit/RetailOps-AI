# Standard Operating Procedure: Returns & Exchanges

## 1. Eligibility & Conditions
- Customers may return or exchange items within 15 days of the in-store purchase date or online delivery date.
- Returned items must be unused, unwashed, and undamaged, with all original tags, labels, and packaging intact.
- A valid proof of purchase (original invoice/bill or order confirmation) is required for standard returns.
- **Missing Receipt Escalation:** Returns requested without a valid receipt cannot be processed by cashiers and must be escalated to the Store Manager.

## 2. Non-Returnable Items
- For hygiene, safety, or pricing reasons, the following categories cannot be returned or exchanged unless found defective at delivery: innerwear, lingerie, socks, cosmetics, personal-care products, FMCG products, gift cards/vouchers, altered/customized items, and EOSS/clearance merchandise.

## 3. Damaged or Defective Products
- Customers reporting defective, damaged, or incorrect online deliveries must contact the store/support within 48 hours of delivery for a full refund or replacement upon verification.
- **In-Store Damaged Returns:** Any damaged item brought to the counter requires physical inspection and explicit approval from the Store Manager before a refund or replacement is issued.

## 4. Refund Methods & Bundled Offers
- In-store purchases are eligible for exchanges or store credit; cash refunds are only issued where required by law or at management's discretion.
- Online purchase refunds are credited to the original payment method within 7–10 business days after inspection.
- **Promotional & B2G1 Returns:** If an item bought under a discount or "Buy 2 Get 1" (B2G1) offer is returned, the discount is prorated and the benefit recalculated. If returning an item breaks the B2G1 qualifying set, the value of the free item (always the lowest-priced item) is deducted from the refund, or the free item must be returned. Used promotional coupons are voided and not re-issued upon return.
