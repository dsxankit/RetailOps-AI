# Standard Operating Procedure: Inventory & Stock Management

## 1. Receiving Shipments
- All incoming distribution center shipments must be unloaded in the designated receiving bay and cross-checked against the digital ASN (Advance Shipping Notice) within 4 hours of arrival.
- If carton counts differ from the ASN, the Receiving Associate must log a discrepancy ticket in the ERP system and get the Store Manager's signature before accepting the delivery.

## 2. Damaged Stock (In-Transit vs. In-Store)
- Items found damaged inside sealed transit cartons must be tagged as "Transit Damage" and placed in the quarantine bin within 24 hours.
- Merchandise damaged on the sales floor must be scanned out under the "Floor Shrinkage" code during evening closing and moved to the backroom defective rack.

## 3. Stock Audits & Replenishment
- Fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG) and seasonal apparel displays must be replenished twice daily (10:00 AM and 4:00 PM).
- High-value apparel and shrinkage-prone accessories undergo mandatory cycle counting every Friday prior to store opening.
