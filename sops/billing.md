# Standard Operating Procedure: Billing, Coupons & Vouchers

## 1. Gift Cards & Vouchers
- Gift vouchers can only be redeemed against the predefined minimum shopping value and must be redeemed in full in a single transaction (partial redemption is not allowed).
- Vouchers cannot be exchanged for cash or cheque, and lost or stolen vouchers will not be replaced.

## 2. Promotional Coupons
- Digital (SMS/WhatsApp) and physical coupons are linked to the customer's registered mobile number and are valid only within the printed validity window.
- Only one coupon may be applied per transaction, and coupons cannot be clubbed with any other active offer or promotion.
- Physical coupons that are mutilated, defaced, or tampered with may be rejected at the Store Manager's discretion.
- **Category Exclusions:** Coupons are valid on Home-Mart categories but do not apply to partner-brand purchases (e.g., Snapup, Kirana), gift cards, FMCG, cosmetics, innerwear, or EOSS/clearance items.

## 3. Billing Discrepancies & Price Overrides
- Cashiers may not manually override item prices at the POS terminal.
- If a shelf tag displays a lower price than the POS scan, the cashier must call a floor supervisor to verify the tag and authorize a POS override.
